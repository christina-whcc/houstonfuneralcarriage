Houston Funeral Carriage

Houston is one of the most diverse and vibrant cities in America — a city that honors its communities, its veterans, and its history with the seriousness they deserve. Houston Funeral Carriage, part of the trusted White Horse and Carriage Company, provides horse-drawn funeral processions for families across Houston and Greater Harris County. Whether you are honoring a veteran at Houston National Cemetery, a beloved family member at a neighborhood cemetery, or someone whose farewell calls for something extraordinary — we are here, and we will make it right. We provide the white horse-drawn hearse, black caisson, family carriage, and riderless horse, coordinating directly with funeral homes to make the process as seamless as possible for every family we serve.

Honoring Houston's Veterans

Houston National Cemetery

Houston National Cemetery on Veterans Memorial Drive is one of the most significant military burial grounds in Texas — 419 acres dedicated to the men and women who served this country. Established in 1965 and home to Medal of Honor recipients and veterans of every American conflict, it is a place of profound honor. We are proud to serve families at this historic site, providing horse-drawn processions that give veterans the farewell their service deserves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Houston Families Ask Us

Do you serve Houston National Cemetery?

+
Yes. We serve Houston National Cemetery on Veterans Memorial Drive and are experienced coordinating horse-drawn processions at this historic site. We work directly with funeral homes to integrate seamlessly into any service, including full military honors.

What areas of Houston do you serve?

+
We serve all of Houston and Harris County, including Katy, Sugar Land, Pearland, The Woodlands, Cypress, Humble, Baytown, Pasadena, League City, Friendswood, Conroe, Spring, Missouri City, and Stafford. Contact us with your cemetery or venue location and we will confirm availability.

Do you provide military funeral carriages in Houston?

+
Absolutely. We provide the black caisson, riderless horse, and white hearse for veteran and military services — coordinating with funeral homes and working within military funeral protocol. The riderless horse and black caisson are also available for any family, not just military.

Can a family member ride the hearse?

+
Yes. One family member may ride the hearse itself for what we call the last mile — physically present with their loved one for that final journey. We have had mothers, wives, grandchildren, and children of the deceased ride alongside. If this matters to your family, ask for it.

How far in advance should I book?

+
As early as possible. Some families call months ahead when pre-planning. Funeral homes sometimes contact us 48 hours before a service and we always do our best to accommodate. Custom plumes require at least two weeks notice; a team of two horses requires at least one week.

Do you work with Houston funeral homes directly?

+
Yes, and we prefer it. Working through the funeral director takes the coordination burden entirely off the family at one of the hardest times of their lives. Funeral directors are welcome to contact us directly — we are easy to work with and always show up.

What if the weather is bad?

+
If we can get there safely, we will be there. We have operated in cold, rain, and difficult conditions because we understand that a funeral cannot be rescheduled lightly. In over ten years of service across Texas, we have had to cancel or reschedule extremely rarely — and never from our end.

Can we customize the carriage?

+
Yes. Custom colored plumes, flag draping, and carriage decorations are all available. We encourage families to provide their own decorations — passed through the funeral director to us at setup — so the result reflects exactly their vision. Whatever matters to your family, we will do our best to make it happen.
